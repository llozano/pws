## Motivation

Device Fingerprinting via GPU & Hardware Error Detection

**The Potential Well Strategy** maps the raw, un-normalized magnitude of mathematical calculation errors—computed across background web workers and hardware-accelerated graphics pipelines—directly into a spatial potential energy grid. This preserves absolute precision drift, which correlates directly with physical hardware and microarchitectural states:

- Isolating Thermal Overclocking & Voltage Pitfalls: When specific clusters of GPU execution units or shader pipelines are pushed or operating under thermal stress, their internal transistors experience micro-fluctuations. The engine captures this as sudden spatial energy barrier spikes across the coordinate grid.
- Locating Corrupted VRAM Blocks: If a specific sector of a device's memory contains a failing transistor or structural wear, mathematical operations reading from that sector experience distinct precision anomalies. The strategy locks this down to persistent, repeating topographical anomalies in the energy grid.
- Multi-Probe Composite Profiling: Beyond the quantum error grid, the engine combines high-precision WebGL shader probes, WebGPU matrix execution hashes, canvas rasterization artifacts, and audio synthesis signatures.
- True Hardware Differentiation: Because it retains absolute error features alongside microarchitectural limits and extension profiles, the resulting SHA-256 fingerprint distinguishes between enterprise-grade accelerators, consumer GPUs, and mobile architectures with high fidelity.

# or
